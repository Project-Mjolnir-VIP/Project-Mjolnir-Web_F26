Conquering New Trails in NYC

How Project Mjolnir is unlocking off-road trails and outdoor freedom for adaptive athletes across New York City parks.

Access to rugged outdoor environments has historically been limited for wheelchair users, restricting recreational mountain biking to specialized, highly expensive proprietary setups. Project Mjolnir set out to change this paradigm by engineering an open-source adaptive mountain bike that combines rugged durability with modular customizability.

Deploying the prototype to Freshkills Park marked a major turning point. Riders experienced firsthand how over 300mm of ground clearance and rear suspension travel transform uneven dirt tracks and rocky terrain into an accessible playground.

"Being able to take corners with full stability and tackle drops without hesitation opens up a completely new realm of outdoor recreation."

With ongoing community feedback and open-source CAD blueprints, the team continues to iterate on component layouts, making mountain biking universally accessible.
