Active Development

Development Timeline

Jan 2023

Approval of Project Mjolnir

In January 2023, Project Mjolnir received official approval and a funding allocation of $15,000 from NYU. This milestone marked the commencement of the design phase for the first adaptive mountain bike.

Feb 2023

Industry Support Secured

Significant support was secured for the project in February 2023. SRAM bicycle components, Specialized Bicycles, and Fictiv contributed to Project Mjolnir, providing assistance with a combined value of approximately $17,000.

Mar 2023

Design Freeze and Manufacturing Preparations

By March 2023, the project reached a design freeze on the concept, and the first part files were sent out for manufacture. This step ensured the team was ready to move from design to fabrication.

Apr 2023

First Assembly in New York

The first set of manufactured parts arrived at NYU's ITP in Brooklyn in April 2023. Over the previous three months, the team had collaborated remotely to finalise the design. Once the parts arrived, the entire bike was assembled in only three days, requiring only minor alterations.

This achievement resulted in the creation of the first adaptive mountain bike in New York City, which also became the first adaptive mountain bike to be ridden across the Brooklyn Bridge. The bike was subsequently deployed to Freshkills Park in July 2023, where wheelchair user Rachel Wallenbach became the first person to participate in adaptive mountain biking in NYC.
