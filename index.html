Milestones

Development Timeline

2023

Approval of Project Mjolnir

Our bikes built at NYU in NYC, Abu Dhabi and in Shanghai were the first adaptive mountain bikes not only to exist at those locations but also to be ridden there.

2024

Industry Support Secured

Working with several adaptive sports organisations in the US, Project Mjolnir was named by Singletrack Magazines most innovative product of the year award.

2025

Design Freeze & Manufacturing

Project Mjolnir bikes were raced at the Slieve Bloom MTB festival in Ireland. The barrier to entry into the sport is the technology and making this affordable allowed people with disabilities from Ireland to be able to compete in the first ever adaptive mountain bike race to be held there, essentially establishing the sport in the country. The team also won the NYU Tandon Research excellence exhibit award.

2026

First Assembly in NYC

The project is on track to reach 40 bikes built. The effort and work of the staff and students at NYU over the project's history has enabled this incredible goal to be achieved. The team also began design, development and deployment of the smaller version of the bike as well as several smaller projects established to address the diverse needs of those with different types of disability.
